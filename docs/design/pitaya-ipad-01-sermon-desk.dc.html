THE LEFT-HANDED DESK
Notebook on the writing side, so his hand never covers the Bible. The rail rides the seam edge, worked by the free hand — Procreate's sidebar logic. Handedness is a setting; the mirror is one tap above.
RECORDING, IN THE CORNER
Start · level · elapsed · pause, quiet in the page header — visible while he writes, never over the paper. Every stroke is timestamped against it; afterwards, tapping a stroke replays the sermon from that second.
REF CARD, JUST DROPPED
Press-hold a verse, drag across the seam — the card lands as an object. Tap it and the reference Bible jumps (see Gen 15:6, washed); the preached text never moves.
INK REFERENCES GO LIVE
“Ro 4:3”, handwritten, gets the quiet dashed underline. A tap — pen or finger — opens the popover or sends the reference Bible there. Sermon audio is Spanish; recognition per notebook is English.
SEAMS ARE FINGER-ONLY
Three snap widths per seam; a Pencil stroke near a divider never re-layouts the desk. Nothing floats over text — the phone's mic dock moved into the rail, and dictation lands at the cursor.
