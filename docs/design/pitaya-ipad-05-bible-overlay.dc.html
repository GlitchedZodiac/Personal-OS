THE ORTHOGONAL RULE
Margin ink always keeps. On the text column: Study interprets (circle = select, tick = accept) and the gesture evaporates; Scratch interprets nothing — every stroke is ink. The highlighter is still the only highlight.
FROZEN, BUT NOT FOREVER
First stroke pins the chapter's layout — aA shows the lock. Unpin to reflow: ink is anchored per verse and moves with its verse, margin notes with their margin.
