PROCREATE GRAMMAR, BORROWED
Two-finger tap — undo · three-finger — redo · pinch zooms the page, never the Bible · QuickShape: hold at the end of a circle / line / arrow and it snaps (see the graft arrow) · hold the rail's color for a quick menu of recent tools · streamline lives in the brush picker.
A PAGE IS OBJECTS, NOT A TEXT FIELD
Ink strokes, typed blocks, reference cards, photos, a recording chip — all on one vertically growing canvas. Ref cards drag, and tap to jump the Bible pane. Every stroke carries a timestamp for sermon replay.
HANDWRITING STAYS HANDWRITING
Recognition writes a hidden text layer — for search and for references like the underlined “Ro 9:6” — on demand (lasso → convert) and once at page close, via one confirm card. “Show text” exists in settings, default off. The lasso menu and confirm card land with deliverable 4.
HIS PENS, HIS COLORS
The brush library leads with the fountain pen and G-pen he actually draws with; the palette opens on his purples, pinks and lighter blues. The six category colors sit one row down — available, never imposed.
