WHICH TOOL AM I HOLDING
The hover chip (see 2a, v. 4) answers before the nib lands: tool glyph + ink dot, 80% opaque, trailing the hover point. It fades 400 ms after hover ends.
TWO MODES, ONE TOGGLE
Toggled from pen settings, echoed in the pane header. Study: pen marks are gestures — tick, circle, drag — that evaporate into system results. Scratch: the page freezes like print and ink stays put. Selection, highlighter and drag work in both.
ONLY THE HIGHLIGHTER HIGHLIGHTS
Six labelled category colors — meaning, not shade. Tap a verse number for the whole verse; drag like a real highlighter for a span. On lift: ~18% tint, 3px edge bar, margin dot, notebook filters. The pen tool never creates one.
CURATE, DON'T INITIATE — IN INK
A pen tick beside a suggested (dashed) mark accepts it; a strike dismisses. The tick evaporates over ~2s; the acceptance persists as a solid bar. Same rhythm as the phone, now at pen speed.
THE FROZEN PAGE, LEGIBLE AT A WEEK
The pin chip + locked aA say why text won't reflow. Ink lives under lines, around words, in the 56px margin — never over the type. System highlights (v.1, v.6) coexist with ink; they are different things and look it. Dark + night surfaces come with deliverable 2.
