- **[2026-10-20] [lane A → watch; ASSIGNED to Lane D by the coordinator] Keep items the server did not store, and chunk the queue drain** — `ios/WatchApp/WorkoutSyncFlight.swift` POSTs the WHOLE pending queue and `removeSynced(pending)` drops every item on any 2xx. The server cannot fix this; the watch must split its drain. Contract: `docs/watch-contract.md` should gain the `itemIssues` key (additive) | pickup: `WorkoutSyncFlight.swift` + `MobileAPIModels.swift WorkoutSyncResponse`.
